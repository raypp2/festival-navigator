Rules that hold everywhere
Priority yt → sc → sp doubles as fallback order, and the last source used is remembered across artists. One player at a time — switching source, artist or screen tears the old one down. No sources → the block collapses to “Nothing to sample yet”. Offline dims it grey; picking never gates on it.
05 · Patterns & rules
Pass — “not for me”
Records considered-and-rejected, distinct from never-engaged. Reversible. Visible to crew but warm — a passed card recedes, never a thumbs-down. Not a sixth pick tap.
✎ 2Notes
Shown inline on the artist page (not a clickthrough). Bubble keeps its sharp lower-left corner — that shape is the notes identity.
Sync dots
ok green · syncing amber · offline grey (a state, not a fault) · error red. One state, one colour, everywhere.
Festival accent
Exactly four places: fest name, active day tab, stage headers, current-fest border. Everywhere else that wants to look “selected” uses brand violet.
Primary action bar
✕NOT
FOR MEPicked ×2★MUST
Not for me · pick · must, in that order, along the bottom on every surface — deck, artist page, desktop focus pane. Never in a header: these are the primary actions. The three segments are always drawn, so “tap again to raise” is legible before the first tap.
Non-negotiables
44px min touch target on every button · focus ring 2px brand @2px offset · motion can never carry meaning (reduced-motion + low-power kill all animation and grain).
Discovery never gates
It ranks and enriches; it is never a prerequisite for picking or the timetable. Offline, sampling pauses while picking stays fully live.

07 · Interaction & motion
Motion never carries meaning
reduced-motion and low-power kill every animation and hide the grain. If a state is only legible while something moves, it's invisible to someone in a field on 4% battery — so every transition below has an instant equivalent, and no signal depends on motion.
fast · 120ms base · 180ms sheet · 240ms ease-out · cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.3,1) reduced-motion → 0ms
Swipe ↔ tap parity
Motion: swipe left = pass, right = pick, up = must; card follows the finger.
Always: the three buttons are the canonical equivalent and are always visible — no gesture is ever required.
Pick cycle
Motion: tap advances ×1→×2→×3→must→clear; aura brightens one step, who-corner tick grows (base).
Reduced: instant state swap — the level is legible from tick height + aura alpha, not the tween.
Pass + undo
Motion: card fades, desaturates and recedes (base).
Reduced: instant recede.
Both: an Undo snackbar persists ~5s — pass is always reversible.
Deck advance
Motion: the acted card leaves, the next rises from the stack.
Reduced: instant replace; the progress bar count is the real signal of "moved on".
Filter sheet
Motion: slides up (sheet token) with a scrim fade.
Reduced: appears in place with the scrim, no slide.
Player tabs
Motion: crossfade between SoundCloud / YouTube / Spotify panes (fast).
Reduced: instant swap; the active tab is marked by fill, not the fade.
Sync dot
Motion: the amber "syncing" dot may pulse.
Never motion-only: the state is also carried by colour + label, so a frozen dot still reads. Offline = static grey.
Aura & grain
Static always — no shimmer or parallax. The convergence signal is the blend, not movement. Grain is hidden entirely under reduced-motion / low-power.
