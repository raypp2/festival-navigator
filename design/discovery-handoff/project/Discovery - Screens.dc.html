Flow change in this work Selecting an artist is no longer an inline toggle on a lineup row. Every artist name in the app now opens the artist page — from the lineup, the timetable, a crew feed or the Discovery deck — and the pick chip lives there. The artist page is an app-wide surface Discovery borrows, not a Discovery screen: it works the same for someone who never opens the deck.
States, any step 4b · no metadata 4c · offline 4d · cold start 4e · zero artists — none of them block picking. Discovery ranks and enriches; it is never a gate.
